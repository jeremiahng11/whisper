Names & voices

One per line: people, companies, products and jargon. Words that sound like one of these ("C trip", "Pricilla") get your spelling in transcripts and minutes. A name you type for a speaker takes its spelling from here.

Names & terms
Built-in vocabularies

Terms that come up in almost every meeting: spelled right in transcripts and minutes, and given to the speech model as hints. Spelled-out letters ("k y c", "K.Y.C.") become the acronym. Your own Names & terms and corrections always come first; everyday words in the lists (fast, bin, pin) are only hints and never changed in the text.
